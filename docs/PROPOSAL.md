# Project Proposal: JobFit-AI

## 1. Description
JobFit-AI is an intelligent career assistant designed to bridge the gap between job seekers and Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). The application uses Large Language Models (LLMs) to analyze job descriptions and user documents (CVs/Cover Letters) to generate a "Perfect Application."

## 2. Problem Statement
Job seekers often struggle to tailor their applications to specific job descriptions, leading to low match scores in automated recruitment systems. Manually rewriting documents for every application is time-consuming and error-prone.

## 3. Solution
JobFit-AI automates the tailoring process by:
- Extracting text from PDFs and Job URLs.
- Analyzing key requirements and skills.
- Reconstructing the user's CV and generating a full business-standard Cover Letter.
- Ensuring language consistency and ATS optimization.

## 4. Target Audience
Active job seekers, students, and professionals looking to optimize their career applications.
